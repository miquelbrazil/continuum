# Annual, vitality, and experience

- Status: draft product concepts.
- Origin: private design dialogue; generalized with AI assistance.
- Related: [RFC 0001](../rfcs/0001-continuum.md), [privacy](privacy-and-projections.md), [provenance](provenance-and-confidence.md).

A Continuum Annual could tell the story of a year through ideas changed,
beliefs revised, unresolved questions, relationships that shaped thought, and
cross-domain links. Quantitative traces may help, but their absence should be
visible. An Annual is a reviewed projection because combining small facts can
reveal new patterns.

“Vitality” describes the *corpus*, not the worth of its subject: coverage,
freshness, perspective diversity, unresolved contradictions, reflection depth,
and trajectory. These dimensions may locate thin evidence and prompt
reflection. No overall human score, completeness percentage, reflection streak,
or default self-optimization narrative. **Not a better self. A more legible
one.** Contradiction and uncertainty can be meaningful.

Archive, Oracle, Timeline, and Graph are complementary ways to navigate. Time
can be a primary axis; graph edges should expose provenance and emergence, not
just semantic similarity. A traversable 3D view is optional, with usable 2D,
search, and readable detail views. Sealed artifacts and literal future time
capsules could add ceremony, with explicit release policy. The product might
feel like a library, archive, or observatory: open, return, trace, unseal,
revisit. **What you preserve can speak again.** The sophistication belongs in
the infrastructure, not the onboarding experience.
