# Concordance

- Status: draft product concept.
- Origin: private design dialogue; generalized with AI assistance.
- Related: [provenance](provenance-and-confidence.md), [privacy](privacy-and-projections.md), [Oracle](oracle-and-memory.md).

Concordance compares two specifically authorized projections to illuminate a
relationship, not to assign people a compatibility score. Possible contexts
include patient and clinician, employee and manager, partners, or candidate and
constituent. A comparison may find **aligned**, **complementary**,
**different**, **potentially conflicting**, **unknown**, or **insufficient
evidence** areas. The useful result is often a question for the participants.

Keep **evidence confidence**, **concordance**, and **coverage** separate.
Apparent agreement from sparse evidence is weak; difference is not failure;
uncertainty should remain visible. Neither party's projection authorizes
exposing the other's private evidence. Both consent, purpose, and correction
paths matter. A graph could show relationship topology, but the graph must
respect both policy boundaries.

For public figures, keep candidate-supplied material separate from an
independent public-evidence corpus of statements, votes, speeches, and campaign
material. Model positions descriptively across time, including changes and
contradictions; do not turn Concordance into default rankings or endorsements.
