# Intellectual lineage: research leads

- Status: draft research map; comparisons require primary-source verification before public claims.
- Origin: private design dialogue; generalized with AI assistance.
- Related: [RFC 0001](../rfcs/0001-continuum.md), [Oracle and memory](oracle-and-memory.md), [attestations](portable-attestations.md).

| Prior-art family | Question to study |
| --- | --- |
| Memex; MyLifeBits | Lifetime capture, linking, and retrieval. |
| Solid / Pods; MyData; Databox; Open Humans | Personal storage, consent, portability, and selective sharing. |
| Personal Knowledge Graphs; W3C PROV; nanopublications | Entity relationships, claim provenance, and uncertainty. |
| Verifiable Credentials / Digital Credentials | Portable institutional assertions and verification. |
| UMA; ODRL; Contextual Integrity | Permissions, usage conditions, and appropriate information flow. |
| MemGPT / Letta; Mem0 | AI memory selection and update across conversations. |
| Plaxo | The person as canonical source for their own contact representation; synchronization outward and the social-invitation consent problem. |

The Plaxo analogy is particularly useful: replace each service's stale
approximation of a person with a canonical, evolving representation and
authorized projections. The caution is equally central: nomination must not
cause spam, contact without permission, or involuntary enrollment. This table
is a reading map, not a verified claim that any project implements or lacks a
particular feature.
